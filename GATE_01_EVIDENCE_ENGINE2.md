| Faculty question asked at Decision level, and the answer given   | Question: How does Engine 2 guarantee that ranking scores remain mathematically bounded (Rubric B2) and provide a verifiable audit trail (Rubric B3) when travelers configure custom weights?    Answer: Engine 2 enforces mathematical boundedness through a strict linear convex combination: all four input dimensions (ratings out of 5.0, popularity out of 100, binary category preference, and proximity scaled to 500 km) are normalized to [0.000, 1.000]. Before computation, the weight validation rule confirms that w1 + w2 + w3 + w4 = 1.000 and wi >= 0.0. Because a convex combination of normalized values cannot exceed 1.0 or drop below 0.0, the resulting score is mathematically guaranteed to remain within [0.000, 1.000]. Furthermore, every ranked card outputs a complete breakdown object recording raw values, normalized components, weights applied, and the arithmetic formula string, providing 100% mathematical auditability.   |
